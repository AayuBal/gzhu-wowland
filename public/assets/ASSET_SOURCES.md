# Visual asset sources

- `gzhu-official-logo.png`: Guangzhou University's official site header logo, sourced from [gzhu.edu.cn](https://www.gzhu.edu.cn/images/logo.png).
- `gzhu-campus-lake-source.jpg`, `gzhu-campus-teaching-source.jpg`, `gzhu-campus-gate-source.jpg`: official campus images for “一塔湖图全景”、“教学区全景图” and “正门”, respectively, from the university's [campus scenery page](https://www.gzhu.edu.cn/xxgk/xyfj.htm).
- `gzhu-campus-lake-pixel.png`, `gzhu-campus-teaching-pixel.png`, `gzhu-campus-gate-pixel.png`: generated pixel collage illustrations based on those official campus images and the supplied WowLand pixel-art materials.
- WowLand mascot, wordmark, and pixel title assets were already included in this project under `public/assets/`.
- `official_cloud_join.png`: WowLand official 3D/pixel cloud cutout from the supplied visual materials (`01_官方物料与设计资产/官方提取矢量与透明PNG资产/06_官方3D_云朵Join.png`).
- `wechat_lead_qr.jpg`: WeChat contact QR image supplied by the site owner for the public community contact section.
